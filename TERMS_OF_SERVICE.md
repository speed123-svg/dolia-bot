- Join and leave voice channels.
- Search for music or other audio using a song name, search query, or supported URL.
- Stream audio through third-party services and playback infrastructure.
- Show playback status, queue information, track titles, requesters, and control buttons in Discord messages.
- Respond to slash commands such as `/play`, `/pause`, `/resume`, `/skip`, `/stop`, `/queue`, `/volume`, `/loop`, `/nowplaying`, and `/help`.
