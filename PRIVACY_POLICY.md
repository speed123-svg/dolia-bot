# Dolia Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dolia ("the Bot"), a Discord music bot, collects, uses, shares, and retains information when you invite, configure, or use the Bot in a Discord server.

Dolia is not created by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Discord Inc. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own terms and privacy policy.

## 1. Information the Bot Processes

The Bot processes only the information needed to provide music playback, queue controls, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. Depending on how you use the Bot, this may include:

- Discord user IDs and display names.
- Discord server IDs, channel IDs, and message IDs.
- Slash command names, command options, button interactions, and modal inputs.
- Music search queries, supported URLs, selected tracks, track titles, track authors, track durations, source names, thumbnails, and playback status.
- Queue state, loop mode, volume setting, requester information, and whether a user is in a voice channel with the Bot.
- Temporary Discord voice connection data needed to connect the Bot to a voice channel.
- Technical log data such as command errors, playback errors, guild IDs, user IDs, channel IDs, track titles, source names, and timestamps.

The Bot requests Discord's message content and member intents for Discord API access, but its current music features are built around slash commands and component interactions. The Bot does not intentionally store ordinary chat message content.

## 2. Information the Bot Does Not Ask For

The Bot does not ask for Discord passwords, login tokens, payment card information, government ID numbers, health information, or other sensitive account credentials.

Do not send those credentials to the Bot or to anyone claiming to operate it.

## 3. How Information Is Used

The Bot uses information to:

- Respond to slash commands and component interactions.
- Search for requested audio and show search results.
- Join voice channels and play audio.
- Build and manage the current queue, playback controls, requester labels, and now-playing messages.
- Keep playback controls limited to users in the relevant voice channel when needed.
- Diagnose errors, connection issues, search failures, and playback problems.
- Protect the Bot, servers, and users from abuse, spam, misuse, or technical disruption.

The Bot does not sell personal data. The Bot operator does not use Bot data for targeted advertising or to train artificial intelligence models.

## 4. Temporary Playback State

Most playback information is temporary and kept in memory while the Bot is running. This includes the current track, queue, requester information, playback status, loop mode, volume setting, and message references for the Bot's own playback panel.

This temporary state is normally cleared when playback stops, the queue is cleared, the Bot leaves a voice channel, the Bot restarts, or the Bot is removed from a server.

## 5. Logs and Retention

The Bot may create technical logs for troubleshooting and reliability. Logs may include Discord IDs, command context, search queries, track titles, source names, and error details.

Logs are retained only as long as reasonably needed for operating, debugging, securing, and improving the Bot, unless a longer period is required to investigate abuse, comply with law, or protect the Bot and its users.

## 6. Sharing and Third-Party Services

The Bot may share or transmit limited information when needed to provide its features:

- Discord receives and processes information according to Discord's own terms and privacy policy.
- Playback infrastructure, such as Lavalink, receives the information needed to search for and stream requested audio.
- Third-party audio or media services, such as YouTube or other supported providers, may receive search queries, URLs, or playback-related requests needed to locate or stream requested content.
- Hosting, logging, and infrastructure providers may process technical data needed to run the Bot.
- Information may be disclosed if required by law, to enforce the Bot's Terms of Service, to investigate abuse or security issues, or to protect users, servers, the Bot, or the Bot operator.

The Bot does not sell personal data to third parties.

## 7. Server Administrator Controls

Server owners and administrators control whether the Bot is invited to and kept in their Discord server. They can remove the Bot or change its permissions through Discord's server integration and role management settings.

Removing the Bot from a server stops new processing for that server, except for any limited technical logs that may already exist.

## 8. Your Privacy Choices and Requests

You may request access to or deletion of information associated with your use of the Bot. Because most playback data is temporary, there may be little or no stored information to provide or delete by the time a request is made.

For requests, contact the Bot owner through the support method listed on Dolia's Discord application profile or open an issue at https://github.com/speed123-svg/dolia-bot/issues. Include enough detail to identify the relevant Discord user, server, and approximate time period, but do not include passwords, tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

Server administrators may also remove the Bot from their server at any time.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for use only by people who are allowed to use Discord and meet Discord's minimum age requirements in their country. The Bot is not intended to knowingly collect information from anyone who is not allowed to use Discord.

## 10. Security

The Bot operator uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect Bot data. No online service can guarantee perfect security, and Discord, hosting providers, playback providers, and other third-party services are responsible for their own systems.

If you believe the Bot or related data has been compromised, contact the Bot owner using the contact method in Section 8.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the "Last updated" date above.

Your continued use of the Bot after an updated Privacy Policy is posted means you acknowledge the updated policy. If you do not agree with the updated policy, stop using the Bot and remove it from any server you manage.

## 12. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or reports, contact the Bot owner through the support method listed on Dolia's Discord application profile or open an issue at https://github.com/speed123-svg/dolia-bot/issues.
